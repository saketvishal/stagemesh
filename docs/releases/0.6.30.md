# StageMesh 0.6.30

StageMesh 0.6.30 fixes the planning lifecycle exposed after Caventra task 138
successfully passed the 0.6.29 challenger schema boundary. The independent
challenge was valid and saved, but the task could not apply its findings because
the generated plan had already consumed the former single-revision allowance.

## Bounded planning convergence

- A generated planning lineage may now perform at most two automatic revisions.
  The limit is a hard ceiling, not an open-ended provider loop.
- Revision rounds are derived from the durable `from_spec_hash` to
  `to_spec_hash` audit chain. Existing tasks with one completed revision resume
  at round two after upgrading; no artifact deletion or manual ledger edit is
  required.
- Each revision intent and completion records its round and the two-round limit.
- Follow-up challengers are explicitly asked to perform a convergence review:
  unresolved contract requirements, impossible declared scope, and non-runnable
  acceptance checks may still block, while optional enhancements and task
  expansion may not restart ideation.
- A second rejected revision remains visible with its saved findings and does
  not dispatch again on an identical rerun.

## Regression evidence

- Synthetic tests cover convergence on the second revision, persistent
  disagreement stopping at exactly two revisions, and resuming a persisted
  one-revision state after the runtime upgrade.
- The installed-wheel development journey now requires two planner revisions,
  three independent challenge passes, and six audited role-protocol corrections
  before it can report all 18 steps complete.

Focused planning tests, the exact repository quality gate, the clean
installed-wheel journey, hosted Linux and Windows regressions, attribution
validation, and protected-main review remain required before publication.
